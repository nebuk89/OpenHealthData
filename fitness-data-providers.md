# Top Fitness Data Providers

**Baseline date:** 5 August 2026

This is the initial provider list for the OpenHealthData audit. It focuses on the companies and
ecosystems that collect meaningful consumer fitness, recovery, sleep, or metabolic data.

## What "top 20" means

This is an **audit-priority list**, not a strict ranking by unit sales. No single credible market
table spans smartwatches, sports watches, rings, screenless trackers, connected fitness
equipment, sleep systems, activity platforms, and continuous glucose monitors.

The list balances market presence, installed base, breadth of data collected, importance as a
fitness-data ecosystem, and coverage of the major product categories.

For market context, [IDC's Q1 2026 wearable data](https://www.idc.com/promo/wearablevendor/)
places Apple, Huawei, and Xiaomi as the three largest smartwatch vendors by unit share. It also
describes Oura as the anchor of the growing smart-ring category.
